# Candidates — One Word for the Boss

Bounty: exactly one word describing the boss (max 30 characters, no spaces).
Judge: pairwise comparison, criteria in order — (1) funniest, (2) truest about
the boss, (3) freshest. With 815 submissions already on the board, generic
words ("micromanager", "tyrant", "workaholic", "visionary", "demanding",
"bossy", "perfectionist") are certainly used by hundreds of entries, so every
candidate below is a coined or uncommon single word with an unexpected angle.

## Winner: Meetingizer (11 characters)

Why it won: it is the funniest (a coined -izer word that turns a universal
workplace pain into a job title), the truest (every boss's signature move is
turning a two-line question into a meeting), and the freshest (not a plain
adjective, not a tired -aholic/-preneur pattern, and not one of the generic
words hundreds of entries will repeat). It lands in a single read with no
jargon and no explanation needed — exactly what a one-word pairwise judge
rewards.
